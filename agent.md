**Project Name:** CareerLink Somalia

**Organization:** JIT — Jazeera Innovation & Technology

**Project Type:** Career, Internship, Job & Professional Networking Platform

**Target Country:** Somalia

# 9. TOP 7 — CORE UNIQUE FEATURE

## IMPORTANT

Top 7 does NOT mean seven system features.

Top 7 means **seven important professional people/contacts that a user chooses to showcase on their profile.**

This is one of the unique features of CareerLink Somalia.

## How Top 7 works

Every eligible user can select up to seven people from their professional network.

Example:

### Ahmed's Top 7

1. Mohamed — Software Engineer
2. Ayaan — UI/UX Designer
3. Hassan — Product Manager
4. Fatima — HR Manager
5. Abdi — Cybersecurity Specialist
6. Maryan — Marketing Specialist
7. Yusuf — Entrepreneur

When another person visits Ahmed's profile, they can see:

**Ahmed's Top 7**

The purpose is to showcase important professional relationships.

---

# 10. TOP 7 RULES

The system should enforce:

* Maximum of 7 people.
* A user cannot add the same person twice.
* Users should select connections from their available professional network.
* Removing a person should immediately update Top 7.
* Users can reorder their Top 7.
* Users can replace an existing Top 7 member.
* Top 7 can be public or private depending on privacy settings.
* A user cannot add themselves.
* Blocked users should not appear as selectable connections.

* Unique owner + connection.
